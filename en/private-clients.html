Your Legal Partner in Italy

Legal assistance for international entrepreneurs, investors and families establishing their personal, business and financial interests in Italy.

Who we act for

Individuals and families who choose Italy.

International entrepreneurs, investors, families and individuals planning to move their residence, or part of their interests, to Italy.

Each matter is handled in confidence through a single point of contact, who coordinates the professionals required in Italy and, where needed, in the client’s home country.

  2. 02

The new-residents regime (Article 24-bis TUIR)

Article 24-bis of the Italian Income Tax Code allows individuals who transfer their tax residence to Italy and meet the statutory requirements to opt for a substitute tax on income arising abroad. The regime has specific eligibility conditions, effects and limits, and may be amended by the legislator.

This information is general in nature. The specific tax assessment of each situation is carried out with qualified tax advisers, with whom the firm coordinates on the legal aspects.
