Investing in Italy

Investing in Italy

Legal Advisory for Private Investors

The investment

From the initial assessment to a possible exit.

For private investors looking to acquire a stake, start a business or make an investment in Italy: legal counsel that accompanies the transaction at every stage.

Meetings are held in English or Italian, in person or by video call.

Stages of the transaction

  1. 01

    Acquiring shareholdings

    Legal due diligence on the target, negotiation of the purchase agreement, warranties and conditions.

  2. 02

    Investment vehicles

    Choosing between direct ownership, an Italian company or a holding structure; incorporation and articles of association.

  3. 03

    Real estate

    Property investments: due diligence, purchase and lease agreements, protecting the investment.

  4. 04

    Contracts

    Agreements with co-investors, directors, suppliers and lenders.

  5. 05

    Governance

    Shareholders’ agreements, voting and information rights, board appointments and conflict management.

  6. 06

    Exit

    Transfer restrictions, pre-emption, drag-along and tag-along rights, withdrawal: the exit is planned at the entry.

The first meeting

What to prepare

No documents or confidential details of the transaction are needed for the first meeting.

  • The objective and time horizon of the investment
  • The sector or type of transaction
  • Who is investing: an individual, a company or a foreign vehicle
  • Any partners or counterparties already identified
  • Advisers already involved

What the firm does not do

The firm does not provide financial advice or investment recommendations: it deals with the legal aspects of the transaction.

Specialist tax advice is provided by tax advisers with whom the firm coordinates; matters governed by foreign law are handled with lawyers qualified in the relevant jurisdictions.
