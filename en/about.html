About

Fabio Valenziano

Business Lawyer | International Legal Counsel

Fabio Valenziano is an Italian lawyer who assists companies, entrepreneurs and investors in managing complex legal matters, with particular attention to corporate and commercial law, tax litigation, employment relations and international transactions.

Over more than twenty years of professional practice he has gained experience in assisting companies and managing disputes, combining the practice of law with a direct knowledge of economic and entrepreneurial dynamics.

His career is marked by an international orientation, developed through professional experience, institutional initiatives and relationships with different economic and cultural environments.

His approach favours a strategic view of legal issues: not only the handling of the dispute, but an understanding of the client’s economic objectives, organisational needs and asset interests.

The firm provides legal assistance to companies, investors and private clients, including in relation to cross-border interests and relationships, coordinating where necessary with professionals from other disciplines and jurisdictions.

  • Professional experienceOver twenty years in practice: corporate law, tax law, complex disputes.
  • Knowledge of the business worldDirect knowledge of economic and entrepreneurial dynamics.
  • International dimensionInternational initiatives and missions, including those connected with the United Nations.
  • EducationLaw degree; second-level Jean Monnet master’s degree.
