Fabio Valenziano

Avv. Fabio Valenziano — Business Lawyer | International Legal Counsel

Profile

Fabio Valenziano is a business lawyer. For more than twenty years he has practised business law and litigation, advising companies, entrepreneurs and investors on corporate and commercial matters, disputes with the tax authorities and transactions with an international dimension.

Alongside his legal practice he has gained first-hand experience of how businesses and markets work, through numerous legal and management roles in Italy and abroad. This dual perspective shapes his approach: first understand the client’s economic and organisational goals, then choose the legal tool.

His education had an international focus from the outset. He works mainly in Italy and Germany and, a keen traveller, has come to know many countries through work and personal interest — a familiarity with different cultures and systems that he brings to his work with international clients.
