Business & Corporate Law

Legal Advisory for Entrepreneurs & Business Owners

Legal counsel for entrepreneurs and business owners

The person behind the business

Ongoing, coordinated legal counsel.

Ongoing, coordinated legal counsel for the people who lead and control businesses.

A business owner’s decisions affect the company, the other shareholders, the family and personal wealth all at once. The firm acts as a constant point of reference for the owner and, where needed, coordinates the other professionals involved.

Areas of work

  1. 01

    Personal counsel to the business owner

    A trusted legal adviser for the decisions the owner takes personally.

  2. 02

    Protecting your position as a shareholder

    Safeguarding the owner’s position as a shareholder: agreements, rights, holdings and role in governance.

  3. 03

    Disputes between shareholders

    Preventing and managing disagreements between partners, from confidential negotiation to litigation.

  4. 04

    Directors’ liability

    Managing the personal exposure of those who run the company towards the company, shareholders, creditors and the tax authorities.

  5. 05

    Structuring and protecting shareholdings

    Organising equity holdings, holding companies and tools to protect shareholdings.

  6. 06

    Generational succession

    Planning succession in the business and the role of family members, coordinating the other advisers involved.

  7. 07

    Handling commercial disputes

    Assessing and managing disputes that directly involve the owner and the business.

  8. 08

    Personal wealth and business assets

    Defining and protecting the boundary between personal, family and business assets.

How the firm works

  1. 01

    Continuity

    A long-term relationship with an adviser who knows the owner, the family and the business.

  2. 02

    Coordination

    The firm works alongside the accountant, the notary and the other advisers already close to the owner.

  3. 03

    Confidentiality

    Personal and corporate matters handled with discretion and under professional secrecy.
