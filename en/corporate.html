Business & Corporate Law

Ongoing assistance to companies, commercial contracts, governance, shareholder relations, procurement, outsourcing, employment, reorganisations and breakdowns in commercial relationships.

What we do

The business at the centre, the law in its service.

We help companies manage their relationships with employees, trade unions, suppliers and banks, seek funding and finance, and expand internationally.

Our aim is to protect the company’s financial stability through a preventive legal strategy.

Areas of work

  1. 01

    Commercial contracts

    Drafting and negotiating business contracts, with attention to the risks that surface during performance.

  2. 02

    Governance and shareholder relations

    Shareholder agreements, governance structures and the handling of disputes between shareholders and directors.

  3. 03

    Procurement and outsourcing

    Procurement and outsourcing contracts, with a clear allocation of responsibility.

  4. 04

    Employment and industrial relations

    Relations with employees and trade unions, in ordinary management and in times of change.

  5. 05

    Reorganisations

    Corporate and business restructurings, from planning through to formalities.

  6. 06

    Economic dependence

    Protection for a business in a position of strong subordination to a dominant partner, under Article 9 of Italian Law 192/1998.
