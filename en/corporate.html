Business & Corporate Law

Legal counsel to established companies, boards, industrial groups and business owners.

Who we act for

  • Established companies
  • Directors and boards
  • Industrial groups
  • Business owners

Counsel that supports the business both in its day-to-day operations and at decisive moments: from contracts and governance to workforce matters and reorganisations, through to the handling of disputes.

Areas of work

  1. 01

    Corporate Legal Advisory

    Ongoing counsel to companies on commercial contracts, corporate governance, relations between shareholders and directors, the liability of corporate bodies and key business decisions.

  2. 02

    Complex Commercial Litigation

    Representation in court and out of court in disputes between businesses, contractual defaults, corporate conflicts and high-value commercial claims.

  3. 03

    Abuse of Economic Dependence

    Protecting businesses in commercial relationships marked by contractual imbalance, abuse of economic dependence, unjustified termination of trading relationships and potentially anticompetitive conduct.

  4. 04

    Employment & Industrial Relations

    Advising employers on individual and collective dismissals, workforce reorganisations, disciplinary proceedings, agreements with trade unions, personnel management and employment litigation.

  5. 05

    Outsourcing & Supply Chain

    Advice on service and works contracts, logistics, outsourcing of production, joint and several liability, contractor changeovers and relationships between principals and suppliers.

  6. 06

    Corporate Restructuring

    Assistance with corporate reorganisations, extraordinary transactions, transfers of going concerns and the early prevention of financial distress.
