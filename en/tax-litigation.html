Tax Litigation & Complex Disputes

Tax litigation and complex business disputes.

Litigation

A core part of the firm’s practice.

From the tax audit to the tax court, from the commercial dispute to the protection of company assets, the firm handles litigation at every stage.

Managing litigation calls for a perspective that takes into account not only the procedural issues, but also the economic, organisational and reputational consequences for the business.

In tax matters, deadlines are short and strict: it is advisable to contact the firm as soon as an audit report, a draft assessment, a notice of assessment or a payment notice is served.

Tax litigation

  1. 01

    Tax audits and assessments

    Support during tax audits, in the pre-assessment adversarial procedure, and in evaluating notices of assessment and any negotiated settlement.

  2. 02

    Appeals before the Italian Tax Courts

    Representing taxpayers at first and second instance, including applications to suspend the contested measures.

  3. 03

    Tax demands and collection measures

    Reviewing and challenging payment notices, demands and enforcement measures by the collection agency.

  4. 04

    Directors’ tax liability

    Defending directors and shareholders held liable for the company’s tax debts.

  5. 05

    Disputes over tax credits and incentives

    Defence against challenges to tax credits, tax bonuses and preferential regimes.

Business litigation

  1. 06

    Commercial and corporate litigation

    Disputes between businesses and between shareholders, covered in detail on the Complex Business Disputes page.

  2. 07

    Contractual and non-contractual liability of businesses

    Defending businesses in claims for breach of contract and damages.

  3. 08

    Recovery of significant commercial receivables

    Pre-action, payment-order and enforcement proceedings for substantial claims.

  4. 09

    Asset protection in disputes

    Interim measures and tools to protect assets while proceedings are pending.
