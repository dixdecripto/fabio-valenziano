02Practice area

Tax Litigation & Complex Disputes

Tax litigation, tax assessments, directors’ liability, high-value commercial disputes, contractual breaches, asset protection and defence in situations of crisis.

When to act

Act without delay.

  • Notice of assessment
  • Tax collection notice
  • Invitation to the adversarial phase
  • Tax audit
  • Challenge to tax credits
  • VAT, IRES, IRAP and IRPEF assessments

Objective: protecting the taxpayer’s tax position and the company’s assets.

Areas of work

  1. 01

    Tax assessments and audits

    Assistance from the audit through to the notice of assessment, including the adversarial phase.

  2. 02

    Appeals to the Tax Court

    Defence of the taxpayer in tax proceedings at first and second instance.

  3. 03

    Settlement and suspension

    Negotiated settlement of tax claims and applications to suspend contested acts.

  4. 04

    Directors’ liability

    Defence of directors in connection with the company’s tax disputes.

  5. 05

    Commercial disputes

    Complex civil and commercial litigation, including debt recovery.

  6. 06

    Asset protection

    Protection of business and personal assets during a dispute.
