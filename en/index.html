The firm

Studio Legale Fabio Valenziano advises companies, entrepreneurs and investors on complex legal matters, commercial and tax litigation, and domestic and cross-border business transactions.

Its approach brings together litigation experience, a working knowledge of how businesses operate and an international outlook, with particular attention to protecting the client’s economic and financial interests.
